# Hall chimes BOX: interface brand (Ragamuffin Workshop, tech feel)

This applies to the phone page (`page.cpp`) and the BOX screen (`ui.cpp`). It is the **Ragamuffin Workshop** look, the same as the shop kiosk (`Ragamuffin Workshop\Kiosk\Ragamuffin Shop.html`), pushed toward an instrument panel or console. References:

- `Ragamuffin Workshop\Kiosk\brand\emblem.png`: a rose window with 16 cusped petals in the four glass colours, a white hub holding a black CNC spindle, a black ink ring, and an outer ring of alternating red and blue dashes.
- `Ragamuffin Workshop\Kiosk\brand\wordmark.png`: RAGAMUFFIN over WORKSHOP in ornate cream display lettering, with tapered rules beside WORKSHOP.

## Type

- **Headings and wordmark:** `"Cormorant Garamond", Georgia, "Times New Roman", serif`, in small caps or uppercase with letter-spacing.
- **UI text:** `"DM Sans", system-ui, "Segoe UI", sans-serif`.
- **Tech readouts:** `ui-monospace, "Cascadia Mono", Consolas, monospace` with tabular numbers. Use it for values, counters, addresses (0x20), pin numbers, ms, mA, Hz, cents and the log.
- The page must work offline on the BOX's own WiFi, so load no web fonts. Use the stacks above; they fall back to installed fonts.

## Tech feel, kept warm

- **Panels:** `paper` fill with a 1 px `line` border and small radius (6 px). Each title is a small uppercase serif label in `muted` with letter-spacing, like a console module ("OUTPUTS", "SUPPLY").
- **Status lights:** small round lamps (8-10 px) beside things that have a state.
  - green = OK or idle-ready
  - gold = firing, flashing briefly on each strike
  - yellow = warm or warning
  - red = hot or fault
  - dim `line` = off
  - blue = info or MIDI activity
- **Meters:** heat and budget bars are thin (4-6 px) segmented bars on `line`. Use the red/blue dash pattern of the outer ring for the supply budget meter. It is the brand's own motif.
- **Grids:** the sequencer uses hairline `line` cells.
  - A step that's on is a filled gold cell, brighter at higher velocity (60 / 100 / 127 as three gold tints).
  - Beat columns (every 4th step) get a slightly stronger rule.
  - The playing column is outlined in `ink`.
- **The one loud element:** the emblem, small, in the header. On the page, draw it as inline SVG generated by a few lines of JS: 16 petals in yellow, green, blue, red ×4, a white hub with a simple black spindle glyph, and a red/blue dashed ring. On the screen, draw it with primitives at about 18 px.
- **Header:** emblem, then "RAGAMUFFIN WORKSHOP" in small serif caps (`muted`), then the instrument name "Hall Chimes" in cream serif, with the firmware version in monospace beside it.
- **PANIC:** solid `bad` with dark text, the largest button on every tab. Mute is an outlined toggle that turns `bad-soft` with a red lamp when muted.
- **Buttons:**
  - primary = `gold` fill with `gold-ink` (#17120a) text
  - secondary = `paper-2` with a `line` border and `ink` text
  - Touch targets are at least 44 px.
- No gradients, no glow, no shadows beyond a 1 px line.

## BOX screen (RGB565)

- **Colours:** use the same tokens via `lcd.color565`.
  - bg `#0c0b0a`, panel `#151311`, line `#2c2823`, ink `#efe7d6`, muted `#a89f8e`, gold `#c49a45`
  - ok, warn, bad as above
  - glass colours for lamps
- **Top bar:** a tiny emblem (an 18 px rose with petals in the four colours and a red/blue dashed ring), then "HALL CHIMES" in a serif-like font if LovyanGFX has one (else Font2), then the IP in a monospace font on the right.
- **Text:** use a monospace font for numbers and readouts. The built-in `fonts::Font0` (6×8) works for tiny text, and `fonts::FreeMono9pt7b` and `fonts::FreeMonoBold9pt7b` for readouts.
- **Tabs:** `paper` with a `line` border. The active tab gets a gold top rule and gold text. PANIC is always solid `bad`.
- **Lists and grids:** OUTS rows and PROG cells follow the lamp, meter and grid rules above.
